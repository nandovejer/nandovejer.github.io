  1. NM HOME VIDEO LUZ 24VHS

    NM-001

    LUZ

    Design system · 2024

    “Enhance native HTML instead of replacing it.”

    LUZ (Light, Usable & Zero-dependency) is a design system in HTML, CSS and ES modules, with no runtime dependencies. The HTML you write comes out styled with no class, and classes only add what HTML cannot say.

    You adopt it in independent layers: just the tokens, the tokens and the components, or the catalog on top, with search, cookie consent, a language switch and patterns for a landing page, a blog or documentation. A project that already has a design can load it inside one marked region and migrate page by page.

    It brings components from buttons, forms and tables to tabs, modal dialogs, alerts and a video, audio and MIDI player. Each one has a page in the documentation with a live example, the markup to copy, and when to use it and when not to.

    It aims for WCAG 2.2 level AA in its three colour modes, light, dark and high contrast: it works with a keyboard, focus is always visible and every page works without JavaScript. Automated checks run on every change, and the accessibility statement also says what has not been tested yet.

    It is made for people while staying plain enough for an AI agent to write it correctly: the site publishes the closed list of classes, and luz-check reads a project's HTML and CSS offline and says which classes, elements, load order or colours are wrong.

    Every change starts with a failing test, and every file has a size ceiling that nobody raises to make it pass. The colour palette is its own, and everything, fonts included, is under the MIT licence, with no registration and no telemetry.

  2. NM HOME VIDEO NM-002 IKAY 26VHS

    NM-002

    IKAY

    Browser fingerprint · 2026

    “Thirty-three data points, zero permissions.”

    IKAY reads 33 pieces of data that any website gets from your browser without asking for a single permission, and shows them as the file a website keeps on you.

    The data runs from the city your IP gives away and the time zone, which compared with that IP's hints at a VPN, to memory, graphics card, battery, installed typefaces and incognito mode. It also follows your mouse, clicks and keys live, without storing anything.

    Each one comes as a sentence, marked protected or not protected, with what it means for you and the technical detail folded away for the curious. At the end it computes “your ID”, a fingerprint that survives cookies and incognito mode, without storing or sending anything.

    The page looks like a dossier: paper and ink, numbered chapters and entries, and a single alarm red used like a highlighter. Headings are set in Fraunces, reading text in Inter Tight and every value found in Fira Code.

    It is in Spanish, English and Arabic, which reads right to left with no special cases. It has automatic, light, dark and high-contrast looks, with text at 7:1 or more in the last one, works with a keyboard, and no status relies on colour alone.

    It is the modern version of the original IKAY, from 2020, which needed a build and had 16 cards: HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the LUZ tokens, no bundler and nothing to install. The tokens and typefaces are served from the repository itself, with no CDN, and there are no cookies and no analytics.

  4. NM HOME VIDEO ¿Quién canta hoy? 23VHS

    NM-004

    ¿Quién canta hoy?

    Cádiz Carnival · 2023

    “Which groups sing today, and in what order.”

    It answers the question everyone asks during the Cádiz Carnival: which group is on stage at the COAC right now, and when will mine be on. The contest at the Gran Teatro Falla lasts weeks, with one session a night and more than ten groups in each, and the official listing is a long table with no times.

    This site turns it into a page for your phone that tells you which session is tonight, roughly when each group goes on, which one is live and how long until the next.

    Each group's time is worked out from the running order, adding up the length of the performance and the set-up; they are estimates, not the official schedule. Every time is Cádiz time wherever you are, and the night's session still counts as that day's until the early morning.

    Each group shows its category, coro, comparsa, chirigota or cuarteto, its author and its town. A search by author takes you to the group inside its session, and there is a link to the live broadcast on YouTube and another that looks up what the group performed the year before.

    When the site is built, the real content of the sessions is written into the HTML, with its structured data, for search engines and readers without JavaScript. It has light, dark and high-contrast modes, and the last two meet WCAG level AA.

    No accounts, no cookies and no server: React and TypeScript on a LUZ theme of its own, with the date logic kept apart from the components and tested, also against a simulated clock. Moving to a new round means adding the JSON copied from the official listing and editing a single file; the dates come from the JSON itself.

    NM-009

    String Transformer

    Text tool · 2023

    “What you type never leaves your browser.”

    Turns any text into more than 50 formats in eight categories: naming conventions such as camelCase, snake_case or kebab-case, encodings such as Base64, URL, hex or Morse, classic ciphers such as ROT13 or Atbash, SHA hashes, a JWT payload and utilities to sort, dedupe or clean up lines.

    It transforms as you type, tells you when the input is not in the expected format, suggests the format it detects and flips the conversion with one button. It all happens in the browser and nothing is sent to a server; it installs as a PWA, works offline and can also be used as a library or from the command line.
