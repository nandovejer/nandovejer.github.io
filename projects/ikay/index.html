IKAY

Browser fingerprint · 2026

“Thirty-three data points, zero permissions.”

C60NM

A Side A

  1. IKAY reads 33 pieces of data that any website gets from your browser without asking for a single permission, and shows them as the file a website keeps on you.

  2. The data runs from the city your IP gives away and the time zone, which compared with that IP's hints at a VPN, to memory, graphics card, battery, installed typefaces and incognito mode. It also follows your mouse, clicks and keys live, without storing anything.

  3. Each one comes as a sentence, marked protected or not protected, with what it means for you and the technical detail folded away for the curious. At the end it computes “your ID”, a fingerprint that survives cookies and incognito mode, without storing or sending anything.

  4. The page looks like a dossier: paper and ink, numbered chapters and entries, and a single alarm red used like a highlighter. Headings are set in Fraunces, reading text in Inter Tight and every value found in Fira Code.

  5. It is in Spanish, English and Arabic, which reads right to left with no special cases. It has automatic, light, dark and high-contrast looks, with text at 7:1 or more in the last one, works with a keyboard, and no status relies on colour alone.

  6. It is the modern version of the original IKAY, from 2020, which needed a build and had 16 cards: HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the LUZ tokens, no bundler and nothing to install. The tokens and typefaces are served from the repository itself, with no CDN, and there are no cookies and no analytics.
