    Memoirs of a coder who writes

    I am Fernando Muñoz, though almost everyone calls me Nando. I work as a Frontend Tech Lead in Madrid and spend my days between Adobe Experience Manager and Salesforce Commerce Cloud, two giants I am paid to bring order to.

    I believe in HTML that works without JavaScript, in CSS that reads like a book and in zero-dependency tools that will still stand ten years from now. So I build lightweight design systems, demos that respect your privacy and the odd tribute to the Cádiz Carnival.

    I code and I write. Sometimes it is hard to tell one from the other.
